About INSURE AI

INSURE AI is an AI-powered vehicle damage assessment and insurance claim automation system designed to make vehicle inspection faster, smarter and more efficient.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify vehicle insurance assessment by using Artificial Intelligence to analyze vehicle images, identify relevant vehicle regions, assess damage severity and support insurance claim processing.

AI-Powered Assessment

INSURE AI uses a YOLO-based computer vision model to analyze uploaded vehicle images and detect relevant vehicle parts. The detected information is then used by the application for automated assessment and claim processing.
